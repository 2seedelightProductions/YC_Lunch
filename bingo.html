Lunch Card
Bingo

One hour, one card. A live game, played only while the show is on. Your card is dealt from the artists and moments of today's show, with a few who won't turn up at all.

  1. Cards are dealt 15 minutes before the show.
  2. Listen live and tap each square as you hear it.
  3. Yacht Club Lunch (weekdays, noon ET): a 4×4 card, four in a row wins.
  4. Yacht Club Brunch (Sundays, 10 to 2 ET): a 5×5 card with a free anchor in the middle. Five in a row wins, and so do the four corners.
  5. Call Bingo while the show is on, or up to 15 minutes after it ends.

The first call that checks out against the show's log wins the Yacht Club Lunch Bunch swallowtail burgee. Winners are named on the air. Free to play; members only; one card per member per day.
